Session nine with Marcus. He arrived ten minutes early, which is new, and said he wanted to get it out of the way before his shift. The main thing this week was the argument with his brother at their mother's birthday: he described raising his voice, then leaving early, and said he felt ashamed for two days afterwards. He reported sleeping around five hours, waking at three most nights, and drinking two beers on weeknights to get back to sleep, which he says has crept up from one. No thoughts of harming himself — he was asked directly and denies any intent or plan. He mentioned anxiety about the performance review in three weeks and said his chest tightens when he thinks about it. We spent the middle of the session on what happens in his body right before he snaps at people, and he was able to name the tightness in his jaw. He agreed to write down one trigger each day this week and bring the list next time, and to swap the second beer for a glass of water. Next week I want to look at how the shame after the argument connects to the way he talks about his father.
